About NovaBuild

We Help Local Businesses Get Found and Get Calls.

An Oklahoma-based team focused on one thing: getting more customers to your door.

Your Small Business Digital Partner

NovaBuild works with local businesses — painters, electricians, roofers, HVAC contractors, plumbers, and general small businesses — across Stillwater, Oklahoma City, Tulsa, and surrounding communities. We handle the digital side so you can stay focused on the work.

When you work with NovaBuild, you get a team that handles everything: website, local SEO, Google Business Profile, social media, ads, and monthly reporting. No handoffs to strangers, no outsourcing. You know who you're working with and you can reach us when you need something.

We're not here to sell you a package and disappear. We build the whole thing — the site, the Google presence, the social pages — and we keep it running so that when someone searches for what you do, they find you first.

How We Work

Google First

Most customers search before they call. We make sure your business shows up in Google Maps and local search when people nearby look for what you do — that's where the calls come from.

Built to Get the Phone Ringing

Every site we build has clear calls to action, click-to-call, and a quote request form. The goal is simple: turn visitors into calls and calls into booked jobs.

Ongoing — Not Just a Launch

We don't hand you a site and disappear. We handle hosting, updates, and monthly reporting on calls, leads, and rankings so you always know what's working.
